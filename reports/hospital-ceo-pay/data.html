Primary sources

  • IRS Form 990 series downloads: the bulk XML releases every figure was read from.
  • IRS Schedule H (Form 990): the hospital schedule, Part I line 7a, where charity care is reported.
  • ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer: a readable copy of each filing, linked from every system page by EIN and object ID.

Version log

  • 2026.3 · 2026-10-21
    Audit of the full table, started from the list of systems where CEO pay exceeded charity care (98 before review) and extended to all ranked systems. Findings and fixes: (1) parent or regional executives reported on a subsidiary's return were being compared with that subsidiary's charity care; where the filings allow it the system is assembled (Mount Sinai, Adventist Health, WVU Health System, Northern Light, Monument Health, Banner Wyoming, CHI Health Nebraska Heart, Rady/CHOC) and where they do not the filer is excluded from CEO figures with the reason (SSM Regional, Touro, Calvary, Covenant Specialty, St Anthony's, Baptist Southeast Texas, Holy Cross Fort Lauderdale, Maple Grove, Cedars-Sinai Marina, Porter, Otero). (2) The same person was split into several rows by spelling: nine joins. (3) The chief-executive rule now excludes titles marked retired, FRMR or FMR, and a chief executive whose title carries an end date yields to a successor whose title carries a start date or is interim; 40 rows changed executive. (4) Two closed hospitals' wind-down years excluded. (5) Ten specialty filers listed in headline_exclusions.csv. (6) Narratives read for 64 filings with small line 7a; none reports charity care elsewhere. (7) Fifty filings re-parsed from XML: no mismatches. Open limitation: systems whose hospitals file under local presidents appear as separate rows; a shared-officer test found 129 such clusters, listed for the next edition. 1,629 systems, 1,051 ranked; headline 75 of 667.
  • 2026.2 · 2026-10-20
    Parkview Health (IN) assembled from its ten filings: the parent filer alone had shown $84,340 of charity care and a 4,360% ratio. 1,642 systems, 1,074 ranked. Ten rows added to rollup_overrides.csv with the filing evidence. No figures changed otherwise.
  • 2026.1 · 2026-10-18
    Initial release. 2025 and 2026 IRS releases; 19,245 Schedule H filings; 2,306 filers; 1,649 systems; 1,074 ranked.
