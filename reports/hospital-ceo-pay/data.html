Primary sources

  • IRS Form 990 series downloads: the bulk XML releases every figure was read from.
  • IRS Schedule H (Form 990): the hospital schedule, Part I line 7a, where charity care is reported.
  • ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer: a readable copy of each filing, linked from every system page by EIN and object ID.
