Report No. 1 · 2026 edition · all states

Minnesota

In Minnesota, 5 of the 21 general hospital systems over $100 million with a hospital in the state reported more in chief executive compensation than they gave in charity care; nationally, 73 of 655. The median system with a hospital in Minnesota spent 0.62% of expenses on charity care, against 1.06% for all systems nationally. Highest reported chief executive compensation: $5,659,485, James Hereford, Fairview Health Services.

58 nonprofit hospital systems have a hospital in Minnesota, 7 of them filing from another state. A system with hospitals in several states is listed on each of those states' pages with its system-wide figures.
